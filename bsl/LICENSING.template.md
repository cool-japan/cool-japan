## What is free

Non-production use needs no sponsorship, no registration and no key:

- evaluating the {{KIND}}, that is, {{EVALUATION}} in order to
  decide whether to adopt it;
- developing, building and testing the {{KIND}} itself;
- non-commercial research, academic research included, and teaching;
- non-commercial personal projects.

Evaluation has no time limit. What ends it is adoption: once the {{KIND}} is {{ADOPTION}}, the Licensor
regards that use as production use and no longer as evaluation, whatever it
is called, and it is the use a sponsorship covers.

Work on a software project of the Licensor, COOLJAPAN OU, is free as well for
whoever develops that project or contributes to it. A project of the Licensor
is a software project the Licensor publishes in its own repositories, and
work on a copy of it that is done in order to contribute to it counts as work
on that project. A fork that is maintained as a separate product is not such
a project.

## What needs a sponsorship

Production use does. The Licensor regards any commercial use of the {{KIND}} as
production use, that is, {{PRODUCTION_USE_DETAIL}}

The sponsor is whoever uses the {{KIND}}: an organisation or an individual. An
organisation's sponsorship covers its employees, and the contractors working
for it, when they use the {{KIND}} on its behalf. The right to production use
that a sponsorship gives lasts while the sponsorship is active and ends when
it ends.

What a sponsorship covers depends on what it is for and on its monthly
amount, at one of these levels:

- USD 1,500 per month or more for this {{KIND}} covers this {{KIND}} alone. At this
  level, a sponsorship for another of the Licensor's works does not cover
  this one.
- USD 4,500 per month or more covers up to five of the works the Licensor
  licenses under the Business Source License: those the sponsor names to the
  Licensor in writing for that sponsorship. It covers this {{KIND}} when this
  {{KIND}} is one of them.
- USD 12,000 per month or more covers every work the Licensor licenses under
  the Business Source License, this {{KIND}} included.

Licensor's page does not change them.

If the Licensor's page cannot be reached, or none of the ways it lists can be

through that page, and is for the works it names. A commercial licence on
other terms is asked for at the same address.

There is no licence key. The {{KIND}} does not ask for one and does not check for
a sponsorship: the licence is kept on trust.
